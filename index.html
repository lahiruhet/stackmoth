02 / Selected work

Systems in production

Court reservation & payment platform

Multi-tenant booking and payments across two live basketball facilities. A double booking is refused three times over, in the database as well as in the application.

Multi-tenant · Payments · Live

CourtRes lighting automation

Raspberry Pi, Zigbee and MQTT floodlight control, running unattended at two venues since 2024. A light counts as off only once the light itself says so.

IoT · Zigbee · Since 2024

Invitrack receivables

Invoicing and receivables with financial integrity enforced in PostgreSQL rather than the application, and a CI pipeline that proves it on every pull request.

Finance · PostgreSQL · CI

SILK hoardings and banners

Site hoardings and exhibition banners for a Rajagiriya development, authored entirely in HTML and CSS and exported at print resolution by a headless browser.

Design · Print · Installed
