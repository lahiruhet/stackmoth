Booking & payments

Reservation platform

Multi-tenant court booking and payments for a Colombo facility operator, live since 2024. Three-layer double-booking defence in PostgreSQL, multi-merchant payment routing, and an incident subsystem built after real money went missing.

IoT & automation

CourtRes

Scheduled floodlight control on a Raspberry Pi and Zigbee, unattended at two venues. Built around a positive confirmation protocol after the logs revealed the system was reporting successes it had not verified.
